Starting off with the infamous single pendulum, we can use polar

The bob of mass makes an angle $\phi$ with the vertical axis,
since we are using polar coordinates, we have the unit vectors
$\hat{\phi}$ that tracks the bob of mass tangential motion,
while $\hat{\text{r}}$ follows the direction of the pendulum's
length.
